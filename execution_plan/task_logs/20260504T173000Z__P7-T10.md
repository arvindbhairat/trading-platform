## Actions Taken

1. **Added `AdminCallbackFailureRatioBreach` notification type** to `NotificationType.cs` with registration in the `AdminOnly` HashSet.

2. **Created `ApiTelemetry.cs`** with a `Counter<long>` for `order_callback_received_total` on the API-side meter (auto-collected by the API's OTLP exporter).

3. **Added 3 counters to `WorkerTelemetry.cs`**:
   - `order_reconciled_via_lads_only_total` — emitted when LADS promotes a pending intent without a callback
   - `order_unresolved_total` — emitted when an intent times out unresolved
   - `order_orphan_total` — emitted when an orphan trade is detected

4. **Wired callback counter into the API endpoint** (`ExecutionEndpoints.cs`): After successful `UpdateIntentFromCallbackAsync`, the counter is incremented with tags `status`, `action_type`, and `user_class`.

5. **Added counter emissions to `IntentReconciliationService.cs`**: Each of the three LADS reconciliation phases now emits its corresponding counter with `action_type` and `user_class` tags.

6. **Added `GetReconciliationCountsAsync` to `IIntentLedgerRepository` / `MongoIntentLedgerRepository`**: Queries the `intent_ledger` collection for callback-matched vs. LADS-only counts within a rolling 7-day window.

7. **Added `EvaluateCallbackRatioAsync` method to `IntentReconciliationService`**: Computes the ratio `lads_only / (callback + lads_only)`, reads the threshold from sys_config (`orders.callback_failure_alert_ratio`), and writes an admin notification when the ratio exceeds the threshold. Includes a 24-hour cooldown to prevent alert spam.

8. **Wired the ratio check into the LADS cycle** (`LiveAccountDataSyncService.cs`): `EvaluateCallbackRatioAsync` is called once per cycle after all users have been reconciled.
