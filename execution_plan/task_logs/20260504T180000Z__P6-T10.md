## Preconditions

## REQ Coverage Attempted

- `portfolio-risk-guidelines § Position Sizing Models` — Portfolio Heat-Based Sizing (listed as required V1 model).

## Actions Taken

1. **Added `MaxPortfolioHeatPct` to `RmeModuleOptions`** — default 5.0%, mapped from `sys_config` key `risk.default.max_portfolio_heat_pct` (REQ-HEAT-002).

2. **Created `HeatBasedSizingModel`** in `apps/worker/Rme/HeatBasedSizingModel.cs`:
   - Implements `ISizingModel` with `Name = "PortfolioHeat"`.
   - Reads current portfolio heat from `AdditionalInputs["PortfolioHeat"]` (decimal percentage).
   - Computes available risk capacity: `equity × (MaxHeatPct - CurrentHeatPct) / 100`.
   - Computes stop distance from `entryPrice - stopPrice`, falling back to `entryPrice` as a proxy when stop price is unavailable.
   - Returns zero quantity with an advisory message when heat is at or above the configured maximum.
   - Clamps negative heat values to zero.

3. **Registered `HeatBasedSizingModel`** as a singleton DI service in `RmeModuleExtensions.cs` (alongside `FixedPercentageSizingModel` and `AtrSizingModel`).

4. **Created `HeatBasedSizingModelTests`** — 11 test cases covering:
   - Standard sizing with remaining heat capacity.
   - At-max-heat / above-max-heat rejection.
   - Zero current heat (full capacity).
   - Missing heat input (defaults to zero).
   - Missing stop price (falls back to entry price proxy).
   - Custom max heat from options.
   - Large equity values.
   - Small remaining capacity → zero quantity.
   - Negative heat clamping.
   - Name property.
