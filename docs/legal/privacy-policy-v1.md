This Privacy Policy explains what personal data the SignalStack platform ("SignalStack" or "the Platform") collects about you, why it is collected, how it is used and stored, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and the rights you have over it under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP).

This Privacy Policy is presented to you for acceptance at signup as a separate affirmative action, distinct from the Terms of Service and the Phase A Tester Acknowledgement (REQ-LEGAL-008, REQ-PRIVACY-003). It remains accessible from the persistent footer of every page in the Platform.

If you do not agree with how your personal data is handled as described here, do not register or use the Platform.

## 2. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to personal data we collect when you register, log in, configure scans, view or modify your portfolio, place orders through the Platform, link a Telegram account, exercise your DPDP rights, or otherwise interact with the Platform during Phase A. It does not apply to data you provide directly to FYERS, Telegram, or any other third party outside the Platform; those services are governed by their own privacy policies.

## 9. Your rights under DPDP

You have the following rights over your personal data. To exercise any of them, email `[GRIEVANCE_EMAIL]` from the email address registered on your account, stating which right you are exercising.

- **Right to access** — You may request a copy of your stored personal data in a machine-readable format, including profile fields, linked accounts, consent history, and notification history. The Platform also exposes a "Download my data" action in the user settings page that initiates the same request.
- **Right to correction** — You may request correction of inaccurate personal data. Profile fields editable by you directly may be corrected without raising a request. Other fields require a ticket.
- **Right to erasure** — You may request erasure of your personal data, equivalent to account deactivation with redaction. Lawful retention carve-outs apply; we will tell you what is retained and why.
- **Right to grievance redressal** — If you are dissatisfied with how we have handled your personal data or any other matter, you may raise a grievance to the grievance officer (see Section 11). If your grievance is not resolved to your satisfaction within 30 days, you may escalate to the Data Protection Board of India.
- **Right to nominate** — You may nominate another individual to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity. Email the grievance officer to record a nomination.

We will acknowledge access and correction requests within 7 calendar days and complete them within 30 calendar days. Erasure requests will be acknowledged within 7 calendar days and completed within 30 calendar days, subject to the lawful retention exceptions described.

## 12. How we secure your personal data

We apply standard technical and organisational security measures, including:

- TLS 1.2 or higher on every public endpoint;
- encryption of FYERS tokens and other sensitive secrets in Azure Key Vault with soft-delete and purge protection enabled;
- multi-factor authentication enforced on the admin identity at the OAuth provider, validated at each admin sign-in;
- step-up re-authentication required for destructive admin actions;
- a strict Content Security Policy, security headers (HSTS, X-Frame-Options, X-Content-Type-Options, Referrer-Policy, Permissions-Policy), CSRF protection on all state-changing requests, and dependency vulnerability scanning in CI;
- documented business-continuity backups with point-in-time recovery for operational data, retained in Azure India regions;
- audit trails for all sensitive actions;
- a documented breach-response procedure with 72-hour notification to the Data Protection Board and to affected users, exercised at least annually.

No system is perfectly secure. If you have reason to believe your account or your personal data has been compromised, contact the grievance officer immediately.

Notification will be sent to your registered email address and surfaced as a portal banner on your next sign-in. We will not delay notification while we investigate root cause; initial notification may be followed by updates as facts develop.
