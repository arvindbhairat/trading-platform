Please read this disclaimer in full before using the platform. By continuing to use the platform, you confirm that you have read, understood, and accepted these statements. A shorter summary of the same message is shown on every actionable surface of the platform; that summary does not replace what follows.

## 1. What this platform is

This platform is a decision-support tool for individuals who trade listed Indian equities on the National Stock Exchange, specifically within the Nifty 500 universe. Users configure their own scanners (called Signal Subscriptions) with their own chosen parameters, risk model, and stop-loss approach. The platform runs these scanners against historical and live market data and surfaces the outputs — scan matches, sizing values, stop levels, and related advisories — to the user who configured them.

## 2. What this platform is not

This platform is not an investment advisory service, a research service, a brokerage, or a portfolio management service. The platform operator is not registered with the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) as an Investment Adviser, Research Analyst, Portfolio Manager, stock broker, sub-broker, or any other category of regulated financial intermediary. The platform does not author, publish, or distribute research, analyst opinions, stock tips, or buy/sell recommendations. It does not operate model portfolios, leaderboards, or tip-based content.

The platform is currently in Phase A — a private evaluation phase that is invite-only, charges no fees, and is not publicly marketed.

## 3. Nothing here is investment advice

Every output the platform produces — including scan matches, entry signals, position sizing values, stop levels, add and reduce advisories, portfolio heat readings, drawdown alerts, exit advisories, and backtest results — is generated by automated tools running against parameters the user configured. These outputs are informational. They are not investment advice, personal recommendations, or solicitations to buy or sell any security. No user should treat any platform output as advice.

You should not make any trading decision based solely on platform outputs. You should consider your own financial situation, investment objectives, risk tolerance, and tax position, and if necessary consult an independently registered financial adviser, before acting on any information the platform surfaces.

## 4. You are always the decision-maker

The platform does not place orders on your behalf. Every order is initiated by you, reviewed by you in the confirmation modal, and submitted by you through the FYERS API Connect widget using your own FYERS account credentials. The platform is deliberately designed so that no trade occurs without your explicit, final, manual confirmation.

## 5. Market risk

Trading in listed securities carries a real risk of loss, including the total loss of invested capital. Prices can move rapidly, gap past stop levels, and remain volatile during events outside your control. Indian exchanges can halt trading, circuit breakers can restrict transactions, and broker execution can fail. The platform's advisory calculations, stop levels, and position sizing do not eliminate or guarantee protection from these risks. Past performance, historical simulation, indicator readings, and backtest results shown on the platform do not predict future market behaviour.

Commit only capital you are prepared to lose.

## 6. Data quality, delays, and errors

The platform ingests market data from third-party providers. Data may be delayed, incomplete, or incorrect. Historical data may contain errors introduced by the provider, by the exchange, or by the platform's own ingestion pipeline. Live quotes displayed in the portal and on charts may be delayed by a known or unknown interval. Corporate actions such as splits, bonuses, and dividends may not be adjusted in platform data until a later phase. Portfolio figures and reconciliation state may lag real-world broker state.

The platform makes reasonable efforts to validate and reconcile data, but makes no warranty that any figure shown is accurate or current at the moment of display. Users must independently verify any figure before using it as the basis for a trading decision.

## 7. Platform availability and defects

Background jobs — including scans, data sync, and notification delivery — may be delayed or skipped. Notifications may be duplicated, missed, or delivered out of order. The platform operator does not warrant that the platform will be available, error-free, or continuously operational at any given time.

## 8. Your broker relationship

Your trading account, trades, holdings, and funds are held with FYERS directly. The platform integrates with FYERS through published APIs but is not operated by FYERS and is not endorsed by FYERS. Order execution, order status, settlement, margin, brokerage, taxes, statutory charges, and any dispute over executed trades are governed exclusively by your agreement with FYERS. The platform is not responsible for any aspect of broker execution.

During Phase A, the platform's FYERS API integration uses an API application registered to the platform operator personally. This is disclosed in the Phase A tester acknowledgement and will be replaced with a commercial registration before Phase B.
