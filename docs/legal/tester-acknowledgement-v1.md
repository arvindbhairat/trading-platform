# Platform Tester Acknowledgement

**Version:** v1
**Phase:** A (private validation)
**Status:** In-house draft by the platform operator. Pending review by a registered Indian fintech lawyer before Phase B transition, per `REQ-LEGAL-005`. Treat this document as the working Phase A acknowledgement; content may be revised based on legal review with a corresponding version bump and required re-acceptance.

---

## Purpose

This acknowledgement is presented to every user approved during Phase A, before they are granted access to any protected feature of the platform. It is recorded as an accepted artifact in `audit_events` per `REQ-LEGAL-004`. It is distinct from the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, which are accepted as separate affirmative actions at signup per `REQ-LEGAL-008`.

---

## Acknowledgement Text

The body below is what the user reads and accepts. Labels, markdown structure, and headings are preserved when the text is rendered in the portal.

---

By accepting this acknowledgement, I confirm that I have read and understood each of the following statements, and I accept them as a precondition for using the platform.

### 1. The platform is operating in private evaluation

The platform is invite-only, closed to public registration, and does not charge fees. I have been personally invited by the platform operator to use the platform during a closed testing phase. Access may be withdrawn by the operator at any time.

### 2. The platform is not registered with SEBI

The platform operator is not registered with the Securities and Exchange Board of India as an Investment Adviser, Research Analyst, Portfolio Manager, stock broker, sub-broker, or any other category of regulated financial intermediary. The platform is not currently offered as a regulated service. This acknowledgement is specific to Phase A and will be replaced with regulated equivalents when the platform completes registration and enters its commercial phase.

### 3. The platform does not provide investment advice

Every output surfaced by the platform — including scan matches, entry signals, position sizing values, stop levels, add and reduce advisories, portfolio heat readings, drawdown alerts, exit advisories, backtest results, and any other content shown in the portal or delivered through Telegram — is generated by automated tools. These tools operate against the parameters I configured in my own Signal Subscriptions and the risk profile I selected. The platform does not author, generate, curate, or distribute research reports, analyst opinions, stock tips, or buy/sell recommendations.

### 4. I am the sole decision-maker for every trade

The platform never places orders on my behalf. Every order originates from my explicit action, is confirmed by me in the platform's confirmation modal, and is submitted by me through the FYERS API Connect widget under my own FYERS brokerage account. I am responsible for reviewing every proposed action against my own judgment, financial situation, and risk tolerance before acting on it.

### 5. I accept market risk

Trading in securities carries a real risk of loss, including the total loss of invested capital. Past performance, historical backtests, and indicator readings shown in the platform do not predict future market behaviour. I will commit only capital I am prepared to lose.

### 6. The platform is under active testing and may contain defects

During Phase A, features, calculations, data feeds, integrations, and notifications may fail, be delayed, produce incorrect values, or become temporarily unavailable. Portfolio figures, reconciliation state, and advisory triggers may be wrong. I agree to treat all platform outputs as informational only and to independently verify any figure before making a trading decision that relies on it.

### 7. My broker relationship is independent of the platform

The platform integrates with FYERS but is not operated by FYERS and is not endorsed by FYERS. Order execution, trade settlement, holdings custody, margin, taxes, statutory charges, and account statements are governed by my direct agreement with FYERS. The platform is not responsible for any aspect of broker execution, including rejections, partial fills, pricing, latency, or outages.

### 8. Phase A uses the operator's personal FYERS API application

During Phase A, the platform's FYERS integration uses a FYERS API application registered to the platform operator personally. This is a Phase A limitation and will be replaced with a commercially-registered FYERS API application before the platform adds users beyond the current tester cohort or begins charging fees. I have been informed of this configuration.

### 9. My personal data is processed under DPDP

The platform is a data fiduciary under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. My rights — including access, correction, erasure, and grievance redressal — are described in the Privacy Notice I accepted at signup. The designated grievance officer for Phase A is the platform operator, whose contact details are published in the Privacy Notice and on the Contact & Grievances page in the portal footer.

### 10. The platform operator accepts no responsibility for my trading outcomes

No warranty, express or implied, is made about the accuracy, completeness, timeliness, reliability, or fitness for purpose of any platform output. To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, the platform operator is not liable for any direct, indirect, incidental, consequential, or special loss arising from my use of the platform, including trading losses, missed trades, incorrect advisories, or platform downtime. Nothing in this paragraph limits any liability that cannot be limited under applicable Indian law.

### 11. This acknowledgement may change

When the text of this acknowledgement is updated, I will be required to re-accept the new version on my next login before regaining access to protected features. I understand that my previous acceptance of this version will be preserved in the platform's audit trail.

## Acceptance Controls

The acceptance screen in the portal must present these controls, in order:

- [ ] I confirm that I am 18 years of age or older.
- [ ] I have read and accept the Platform Tester Acknowledgement (version **v1**).

Only after both checkboxes are ticked and an explicit "Accept and continue" button is clicked may the acceptance be recorded and the user granted access. The acceptance record written to `audit_events` must include: user identity, acknowledgement version, timestamp (UTC), and the user agent at the time of acceptance.
