## Purpose

This document is the SignalStack platform's internal Record of Processing Activities under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. It enumerates — at operator-internal granularity — every processing activity the platform performs on personal data, the lawful basis for each, the categories of data principals affected, retention periods, the third-party processors involved, and the security measures applied.

This is the source of truth that the user-facing Privacy Policy at `docs/legal/privacy-policy-v1.md` summarises in plain language. When this RoPA changes, the Privacy Policy must be reviewed for whether the change is material enough to require a version bump and refreshed user consent under REQ-PRIVACY-008.

This document is not published to users. It is an internal compliance artefact.

## Processing activities

For each activity: purpose, data categories used, lawful basis, retention, recipients.

### A1. User authentication and session management

- **Purpose:** Verify identity at sign-in and maintain session state for the duration of an authorised portal session.
- **Data categories:** Identity and contact; authentication state.
- **Lawful basis:** Performance of contract (the Terms of Service entered at signup).
- **Retention:** Active session expires 24 hours after issuance (TTL on `sessions` collection); identity data retained for the lifetime of the active account; consent acceptance records retained indefinitely as proof.
- **Recipients:** OAuth providers (Google, Microsoft, Meta) for the handshake itself; otherwise internal.

### A2. FYERS broker integration

- **Purpose:** Fetch the user's trades, holdings, positions, and orders from FYERS; pre-populate order parameters for the FYERS API Connect widget on user-initiated execution.
- **Data categories:** FYERS integration credentials; trading data.
- **Lawful basis:** Performance of contract; specific consent provided at FYERS OAuth.
- **Retention:** Access tokens are day-scoped and superseded tokens retained 30 days for audit; trade ledger retained per REQ-PORT-002 (immutable, archived after 36 months but remains queryable); holdings and positions overwritten in place per sync.
- **Recipients:** FYERS (the broker itself); internal storage in MongoDB.

### A3. Portfolio analytics computation

- **Purpose:** Compute and display average buy, FIFO-derived PnL, equity curve, RME state, drawdown, portfolio heat for the user's dashboard, charts, and notifications.
- **Data categories:** Trading data; platform-derived analytics; scan and subscription configuration.
- **Lawful basis:** Performance of contract.
- **Retention:** Equity curve archived after 36 months but remains queryable; portfolio snapshots overwritten in place per sync; closed positions archived 12 months after close per `data-management.md`.
- **Recipients:** Internal only.

### A4. Signal scanning and entry-signal generation

- **Purpose:** Run user-configured Signal Subscriptions against historical OHLCV data on each EOD pass and emit entry signals to the user who configured them.
- **Data categories:** Scan and subscription configuration; trading data (for portfolio impact assessment).
- **Lawful basis:** Performance of contract.
- **Retention:** Entry signals archived after 12 months; signal-run records archived after 90 days.
- **Recipients:** Internal only.

### A5. Notification delivery (portal feed)

- **Purpose:** Surface platform-generated alerts to the user in the portal notification feed.
- **Data categories:** Notification linkage; scan output; identity (for routing).
- **Lawful basis:** Performance of contract.
- **Retention:** Notifications active in the user feed for 90 days, archived thereafter.
- **Recipients:** Internal only.

### A6. Notification delivery (Telegram)

- **Purpose:** Deliver platform-generated alerts to the user's linked Telegram chat.
- **Data categories:** Notification linkage (chat ID); notification body content (which may reference the user's symbols, positions, advisory state).
- **Lawful basis:** Specific consent (user opts in by linking Telegram and may opt out at any time per REQ-NOTIFY-018).
- **Retention:** Telegram delivery status retained on the notification record per its lifecycle; Telegram itself retains chats per its own policy.
- **Recipients:** Telegram (Telegram FZ-LLC and its global infrastructure).
- **Cross-border transfer:** Yes — Telegram operates globally including outside India. Transfer is necessary to provide the opted-in service.

### A7. Notification delivery (admin operational email)

- **Purpose:** Deliver admin-only operational notifications to the admin via email as primary or fallback channel per REQ-NOTIFY-021.
- **Data categories:** Identity (admin email); notification body content (may reference operational metrics, never user trading data).
- **Lawful basis:** Performance of contract; legitimate interest in operational integrity.
- **Retention:** Email delivery status retained on the notification record.
- **Recipients:** Transactional email provider (Azure Communication Services).
- **Cross-border transfer:** No — Azure India regions only per REQ-LEGAL-009.

### A8. Audit and compliance recording

- **Purpose:** Record all sensitive admin actions, consent changes, phase transitions, sys_config changes, breach detection events, DPDP rights requests, and other audit-critical events.
- **Data categories:** Identity; audit and consent records; admin actions affecting the user.
- **Lawful basis:** Legal obligation (DPDP record-keeping; SEBI record-keeping when applicable in Phase C); legitimate interest in operational integrity.
- **Retention:** `audit_events` archived after 24 months but remains queryable; some specific event types (consent acceptance, manual adjustments) retained indefinitely.
- **Recipients:** Internal only.

### A9. Security monitoring

- **Purpose:** Detect and respond to unauthorised access attempts, brute-force authentication, dependency or secret-scan CI alerts, and suspected breaches.
- **Data categories:** Authentication state; operational metadata; identity (for correlation).
- **Lawful basis:** Legitimate interest in security and integrity; legal obligation under DPDP for breach detection and notification.
- **Retention:** Security-relevant audit events retained per the audit trail; observability logs per the observability vendor's policy (typically 30–90 days).
- **Recipients:** Internal; observability vendor for telemetry storage.

### A10. Grievance and DPDP rights handling

- **Purpose:** Receive and respond to user grievances, access requests, correction requests, and erasure requests.
- **Data categories:** Identity; audit and consent; whatever data is the subject of the request.
- **Lawful basis:** Legal obligation under DPDP.
- **Retention:** Request and response retained indefinitely as compliance evidence.
- **Recipients:** Internal only (the grievance officer and admin); copied to the requester as part of the response.

### A11. Service-notice and re-acceptance communication

- **Purpose:** Notify users when the ToS, Privacy Policy, Tester Acknowledgement, or long-form disclaimer is bumped and require re-acceptance on next login.
- **Data categories:** Identity; audit and consent.
- **Lawful basis:** Performance of contract; legal obligation under DPDP for refreshed consent.
- **Retention:** Re-acceptance records retained indefinitely as proof of consent.
- **Recipients:** Internal only.

### A12. Admin "View as user" impersonation

- **Purpose:** Allow the designated admin to access a read-only view of any user's portal session for operational support, troubleshooting, and platform diagnostics.
- **Data categories:** All data visible in the target user's portal session — identity and contact (display name), trading data (positions, holdings, broker orders), platform-derived analytics (portfolio snapshot, equity curve, RME state), scan and subscription configuration, notification history, and settings. No financial credentials (FYERS token) are transferred to the admin session; the admin views only the data already stored and rendered by the platform.
- **Lawful basis:** Legitimate interest of the data fiduciary in operating and maintaining the platform and resolving operational issues on behalf of users, subject to the safeguards below.
- **Safeguards:** (a) Admin must complete step-up re-authentication (fresh OAuth within the previous 5 minutes) before entering and exiting each impersonation session (REQ-SEC-011). (b) The session is strictly read-only; all write-path API calls originating from an impersonation session are rejected server-side (REQ-ADMIN-015). (c) Every impersonation session — entry time, exit time, and pages visited — is recorded in `audit_events` with the admin identity and target user identity (REQ-ADMIN-015). (d) Impersonation sessions auto-expire after a configurable idle timeout (default 15 minutes, `sys_config` key `operations.impersonation.idle_timeout_minutes`). (e) A persistent high-visibility banner labels every page during the session.
- **Disclosure:** The Privacy Policy served to users must disclose, in plain language, that the admin may access a read-only view of any user's account for operational support purposes and that each such access is recorded. Required by REQ-PRIVACY-012 before the first non-admin user is onboarded.
- **Retention:** Impersonation session audit records are retained as part of `audit_events` per the standard audit trail retention (A8). No separate copy of user data is created or exported during an impersonation session.
- **Recipients:** Internal only (the admin).

### A13. Hosting, storage, and backup

- **Purpose:** Operate the platform's databases, secret stores, configuration stores, and observability pipeline.
- **Data categories:** All categories listed above are hosted on Azure infrastructure.
- **Lawful basis:** Performance of contract; legitimate interest in operational integrity.
- **Retention:** Backups retained per REQ-BCP-001 to REQ-BCP-005 (MongoDB PITR with 24-hour rolling, SQL Server daily/weekly, Key Vault soft-delete 90 days, geo-redundant copies in India South).
- **Recipients:** Microsoft Azure (Central India and South India regions).

## Data principal rights workflow

Email-driven for V1 per REQ-PRIVACY-004:

1. User emails `[GRIEVANCE_EMAIL]` from the email registered on their account, identifying the right being exercised (access, correction, erasure, grievance, nomination).
2. Admin logs the request as a ticket in the admin portal queue, captured in `audit_events`.
3. Acknowledgement sent within 7 calendar days.
4. Resolution within 30 calendar days, with retained-data carve-outs disclosed for erasure requests.
5. Resolution and the artefact (export file, correction confirmation, deactivation confirmation) recorded in `audit_events`.

Repeated requests within 24 hours are coalesced to a single pending ticket per REQ-PROFILE-010.
